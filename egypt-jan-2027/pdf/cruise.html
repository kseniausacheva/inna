Nile charter

Luxor to Aswan · 25–30 January 2027
Private full charter, 14–16 guests

14–16 guests, mostly single occupancy up to 21 persons needing a bed target 18–20 cabins 3–4 h of group work every day on board

This document describes the river section in the detail an operator needs in order to quote: every leg, every stop, time ashore, and what happens on board in between. Where we state a distance or a duration, it is our working assumption, and we ask you to correct it with your own operating data for this vessel.

How to read the times

Times in bold are fixed by monument opening hours, by flights, or by the client, and we plan around them. Times in plain text are estimates and we expect them to move. We do not fix the hour of any event that depends on arrival in port. Please tell us where our assumptions are wrong rather than agreeing to them.

The single question that decides this itinerary

Is the vessel permitted to navigate after dark? Our plan is built so that the Esna lock is crossed while guests are at dinner or asleep. If the queue is six hours, the vessel simply reaches Edfu at 02:00 instead of 20:00 and nothing in the guest programme changes, because the Edfu visit is at 07:00 either way. If the vessel must moor at dusk, we will instead embark at Esna, above the lock, and the vessel transits the lock without guests. Please tell us which of the two applies, and whether an authorised embarkation quay exists at Esna.

2. Two options

Option A — preferred · 4 nights, plus 1 optional

Embarkation Luxor, 25 January, around 11:30. Night 1 moored in Luxor, no sailing. Night 2 at Edfu. Nights 3 and 4 in Aswan. Final disembarkation Aswan 29 January at 05:00, or, if we take the optional fifth night, 30 January in the morning.

Option B — 3 nights

Embarkation 26 January, in Luxor around 10:00 or in Esna around 11:00 depending on your answer on night navigation. Night 1 at Edfu, nights 2 and 3 in Aswan. Final disembarkation Aswan 29 January at 05:00. Please quote both options.

Day 5 · Thursday 29 January · Aswan

Two sub-options, please price both

A1 — disembarkation on Day 5. Early breakfast on board at 04:30, final disembarkation with all luggage 05:00–05:30. Guests leave for Abu Simbel by road and do not return to the vessel. Charter ends. Please confirm the 04:30 breakfast is possible and whether it is included.

A2 — one extra night, our preference. Same early breakfast and departure at 05:30, but luggage and guests remain on board. The vessel stays moored in Aswan all day and the night of 29/30. Guests return from Abu Simbel around 16:00–17:00, change in their cabins, go ashore for a gala dinner at a hotel in the evening and return late. Final disembarkation 30 January in the morning, straight to Aswan airport. Please quote this fifth night as a moored night without sailing, and confirm late return access to the vessel around 23:00.

Why A2 matters to us

It removes the need to send 20 pieces of luggage to a hotel by a separate vehicle while the guests are 290 km away in the desert, and it removes a hotel check-in after an eight-hour road day. If your rate for a moored night is reasonable, this is the version we will take.

6. The working sessions

Three to four hours every day of the river section, for 18–20 people, both under way and at anchor. This is a contractual client requirement and the main reason this charter exists in this form. In full: a dedicated enclosed room, seating with backrests for 20, at least six sockets, air conditioning controlled in the room, and exclusive use during the agreed block with no cleaning, no table laying, no background music and no crew traffic, with service limited to one coffee break served and cleared in a single pass.

We ask for the deck plan, a seating layout for 20, photographs of the room empty and in daylight rather than set for dining, the position of the engine room relative to it, and whether normal conversation is possible there while under way.

7. Events on board

Gala dinner, evening of 27 January. Special menu, decoration, optionally a Nubian ensemble. We will not announce an hour to guests until the master gives us an arrival estimate, and we ask you not to promise one either. Please tell us what is charged in addition to the included dinner, where and when performers can board, and whether their meals are at our cost.

8. Mooring and access

  • Exact quays for embarkation and disembarkation in Luxor and Aswan, and at Esna if that option applies.
  • Whether you expect to moor alongside other vessels in Edfu and Aswan. We will warn our guests; we simply need to know.
  • Road access at the Aswan mooring, since guests leave for Philae by car and return late.
  • Late return access around 23:00 on 29 January in sub-option A2.
  • Who pays port dues, the lock fee and any fuel surcharge, and whether a tug is required.

9. What we ask you to send back

  1. Your own sailing times for the four legs, replacing our assumptions.
  2. A direct yes or no on navigation after dark.
  3. Typical Esna lock waiting time in January from your records, and whether the vessel can transit without guests.
  4. Minimum paid charter period in nights, and a separate rate for a moored night.
  5. Hull price for Option A, Option A2 and Option B, as a single price for the whole vessel.
  6. Deck plan with cabin numbers, how many cabins you can sell in single occupancy, and where the crew sleeps.
  7. The working room: plan, layout for 20, empty photographs, engine room position, noise under way.
  8. Your price in two separate lines: vessel only, and vessel plus shore programme, stating exactly which guides, tickets and quay transfers are included.
  9. What happens, and who pays, if the lock queue costs us a night.
