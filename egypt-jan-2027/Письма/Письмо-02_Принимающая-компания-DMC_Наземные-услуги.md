# ПИСЬМО 02. ЗАПРОС НАЗЕМНЫХ УСЛУГ

*Проект Египет, 23–30 января 2027. La Royal Event.*



| Поле | Значение |
|---|---|
| **КОМУ** | принимающей компании, 2–3 кандидатам одновременно. |
| **ОТДЕЛ** | Incoming / MICE. |
| **ТЕМА** | `Ground services RFP — private group 14–16, Cairo / Luxor / Aswan, 23–30 January 2027` |
| **КОГДА** | в течение трёх дней, после первых ответов от судов. |
| **ЗАЧЕМ** | выбрать одного ответственного партнёра на всю наземную часть. При сбое у вас должен быть один телефон, а не семь. |


**ОТВЕТ ДОЛЖЕН СОДЕРЖАТЬ:**
- цену на человека отдельно для 14 и для 16 гостей;
- модель и число мест каждой машины плюс подтверждение резервной машины;
- режим блокпостов на Абу-Симбел именно на январь 2027;
- разделённые услуги: meet & greet отдельно, fast track отдельно;
- подтверждение микроавтобусов в Эдфу вместо конных экипажей;
- перечень того, что обычно уже входит в судовой пакет, чтобы не платить дважды;
- клиники в трёх городах и порядок медицинской эвакуации с реки;
- референсы по двум сопоставимым группам.

```
Dear Colleagues,

We are organising a private programme for 14–16 guests, 23–30 January 2027, with a fully
chartered Nile vessel. We are selecting ONE ground partner for the whole itinerary and
ask for a written proposal.

SCOPE
- Airport meet & greet and all transfers: Cairo arrival 23 Jan, Cairo airport 25 Jan,
  Luxor arrival 25 Jan, Aswan 29–30 Jan.
- All ground transport in Cairo, Luxor and Aswan, including a SEPARATE luggage vehicle
  on 29 January.
- Russian-speaking Egyptologist for the entire programme.
- Entrance tickets for all sites, priced per person at January 2027 tariffs.
- Motorboat to Philae from Shellal, and separately a felucca for the Kitchener Island area.
  Please quote these as TWO SEPARATE services — they are not interchangeable.
- Full-day private Abu Simbel excursion by road on 29 January.
- Coordination of special-access permits (see separate enquiries).
- On-site replacement capability and a 24-hour emergency contact.

SPECIFIC QUESTIONS
1. Cairo: please separate MEET & GREET from FAST TRACK. What exactly does each include,
   and is fast track issued against a named passenger list?
2. Giza plateau: what is the actual opening time in January, and what does "early access"
   mean in practice — first at the turnstile, or an empty plateau before opening?
   If the latter exists, it is a separate permit: please quote it.
3. Grand Egyptian Museum: timed entry only, via the official platform. Can you secure a
   specific slot for 18 persons, and by what deadline?
4. Abu Simbel, January 2027: what is the CURRENT regime of police checkpoints and organised
   departures? We know the mandatory convoy was abolished, but practice changes — please
   confirm what applies for January 2027 and re-confirm in December 2026.
5. Abu Simbel: realistic door-to-door timing, rest stops, meal arrangements, water,
   and your backup vehicle plan.
6. Philae: confirm the route from a vessel moored in Aswan to Shellal quay, driving time,
   and boat capacity.
7. Kitchener Island: is a private lunch in the botanical garden permitted, in what format,
   and what permit is required?
8. Edfu: we require minibuses from the quay to the temple, not horse-drawn carriages.
   Please confirm availability and price.
9. Which of the above are ALREADY included by a cruise operator's shore package, in your
   experience? We must not pay twice.
10. Vehicles: please state model, number of seats and whether a spare vehicle is on standby.
    We need surplus seats, not exactly 16.
11. Medical: nearest suitable clinics in Cairo, Luxor and Aswan, and the procedure for a
    medical evacuation from a vessel on the river.

Please quote per person for 14 and for 16 guests separately, and state your payment and
cancellation terms. Kindly also send references for two comparable private groups.

Kind regards,
Ksenia
La Royal Event, Egypt
```
