# ПИСЬМО 07. БЛОК НОМЕРОВ В ЛУКСОРЕ

*Проект Египет, 23–30 января 2027. La Royal Event.*



| Поле | Значение |
|---|---|
| **КОМУ** | Hilton Luxor Resort & Spa. |
| **ОТДЕЛ** | Group Reservations. |
| **ТЕМА** | `Group block enquiry — 18 rooms, mainly single occupancy, 25–26 January 2027` |
| **КОГДА** | только если клиент выбрал вариант II. В рекомендуемом варианте I этот отель не нужен: гости ночуют на судне. |
| **ЗАЧЕМ** | закрыть самое рискованное утро поездки. Всё зависит от раннего завтрака в 05:00 и от передачи багажа на судно, пока гости на Западном берегу. |


**ОТВЕТ ДОЛЖЕН СОДЕРЖАТЬ:**
- письменное подтверждение раннего завтрака или боксов с 05:00 26 января;
- гарантию раннего заезда 25 января;
- согласие организовать передачу багажа на судно утром 26 января и кто за это отвечает;
- дату релиза блока и условия отмены.

```
Dear Group Reservations,

La Royal Event (Egypt) requests a group block:
- Dates: arrival Monday 25 January 2027, departure Tuesday 26 January 2027 (1 night)
- Rooms: up to 18, the MAJORITY IN SINGLE OCCUPANCY
- Highest available category with Nile view

PLEASE CONFIRM
1. Availability and rate, single occupancy priced separately.
2. Category names and how many rooms per category you can hold.
3. GUARANTEED EARLY CHECK-IN on 25 January: guests arrive from Cairo around midday and
   need rooms before an afternoon excursion. What can be guaranteed, and at what cost?
   If it cannot be guaranteed, what waiting facilities do you offer for 18 guests?
4. EARLY BREAKFAST or breakfast boxes from 05:00 on 26 January, for 18 guests. Please
   confirm this in writing — our guests depart for the west bank at 05:30.
5. LUGGAGE TRANSFER on the morning of 26 January: guests will leave the hotel at 05:30
   and will NOT return. Their luggage must be collected from the rooms after departure
   and handed over to a vehicle for delivery to a vessel at the Luxor quay. Please confirm
   that you can support this, who is responsible on your side, and the latest time by
   which rooms must be vacated for it.
6. Express check-out arranged the previous evening, so that no guest has to settle an
   account at 05:15.
7. Driving time from the hotel to the west bank sites via the bridge, from your experience.
8. Release date, cancellation terms, rooming list deadline.
9. Payment schedule.

Kind regards,
Ksenia
La Royal Event, Egypt
```
