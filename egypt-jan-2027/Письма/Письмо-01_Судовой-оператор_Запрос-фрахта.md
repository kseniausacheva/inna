**ОТВЕТ ДОЛЖЕН СОДЕРЖАТЬ:**
- схему палуб с нумерацией кают и отметкой, какие продаются в одноместном размещении;
- где живёт экипаж, с подтверждением, что гостевой фонд не используется под команду;
- минимальный оплачиваемый срок фрахта в ночах;
- цену всего корпуса двумя отдельными строками: только борт, и борт плюс береговая программа;
- отдельную ставку за ночь стоянки без движения;
- прямой ответ «да» или «нет» про движение после наступления темноты;
- расчётное время переходов для этого борта;
- план расстановки рабочего салона и фото пустого помещения;
- определение Full Board с указанием первого и последнего включённого приёма пищи.

```
Dear Charter Team,

La Royal Event (Egypt) is planning a private, fully chartered Nile programme for a
high-requirement group of 14–16 guests, 23–30 January 2027. We are comparing a short
list of vessels and would be grateful for a written reply to the points below.

GROUP AND OCCUPANCY
- 14 to 16 guests, the majority requiring SINGLE occupancy. Twin sharing only by
  voluntary agreement of individual guests; the programme cannot be priced on the
  assumption that all guests share.
- In addition, up to 2 tour escorts, 1 Russian-speaking Egyptologist and 1 session
  facilitator may sleep on board. Total persons needing a bed: up to 21.
- Please confirm how many cabins your vessel can actually sell in single occupancy
  under a full charter.

DATES — TWO SCENARIOS
Option A (preferred): 4 nights on board, embarkation Luxor 25 January, disembarkation
Aswan 29 January; plus a possible 5th night moored in Aswan, 29/30 January.
Option B: 3 nights on board, embarkation Luxor or Esna 26 January, disembarkation
Aswan 29 January.

QUESTIONS WE NEED ANSWERED IN WRITING

Capacity and layout
1. Deck plan with cabin numbers, showing which cabins are suitable for single use.
2. Where is the crew accommodated? Please confirm that no guest cabin is used by crew,
   guides or performers.
3. Total number of cabins and maximum guest capacity.

Charter terms
4. Minimum paid charter period for these dates, in nights.
5. Full price of the entire vessel for Option A and Option B, as a single hull price.
6. Separate rate for a night MOORED WITHOUT SAILING (we may need 29/30 January in Aswan).
7. Payment schedule, deposit, cancellation terms, and what happens if the group size changes.

Navigation — this determines which option we buy
8. Is the vessel permitted to navigate AFTER DARK between Luxor and Aswan?
   If yes, under what conditions and with what authority's approval?
9. Estimated sailing times FOR THIS VESSEL, upstream, for: Luxor–Esna, Esna–Edfu,
   Edfu–Kom Ombo, Kom Ombo–Aswan.
10. Typical Esna lock waiting time in January, from your own recent operating records.
11. Who pays the lock fee, port dues and any fuel surcharge?
12. Is a tug required? Who pays for it?
13. Can the vessel transit the Esna lock WITHOUT guests on board and wait for the group
    above the lock at Esna? Is there an authorised embarkation quay at Esna?
14. Exact embarkation and disembarkation quays in Luxor and Aswan.
15. What is your procedure if the lock queue delays arrival by more than 4 hours?
    Who bears the cost of an additional night?

Working space on board — this is a firm client requirement
16. We need a dedicated enclosed space for 3–4 hours of group work EVERY DAY of the
    river section, for 18–20 people. Please send:
    - the floor plan of the proposed room with a seating layout for 20 persons;
    - photographs of the EMPTY room, not of the room set for dining;
    - number and location of power sockets, and voltage;
    - confirmation of individually controlled air conditioning;
    - the position of the engine room relative to this room, and the noise level while
      under way;
    - written confirmation of EXCLUSIVE use during agreed hours, with no cleaning,
      no table service and no background music during those hours;
    - whether tables, a screen or a flip chart can be provided.

Meals and inclusions
17. What exactly does Full Board mean on your vessel: which is the FIRST and which is the
    LAST included meal relative to embarkation and disembarkation?
18. Are early breakfasts from 04:30 and packed breakfast boxes included? If not, price.
19. Are two coffee breaks per day during the working sessions included? If not, price.
20. Is drinking water included, in cabins and on excursions?
21. Which beverages are included with lunch and dinner? Is own alcohol permitted,
    and is there a corkage charge?
22. Special diets and allergies: by what deadline do you need the information?
23. Are meals and accommodation for the Egyptologist, facilitator and escorts charged
    separately?

Shore services — we must avoid paying twice
24. Please quote in TWO SEPARATE LINES:
    (a) VESSEL ONLY — hull, cabins, meals, crew, navigation;
    (b) VESSEL PLUS SHORE PROGRAMME — stating precisely whether guides, entrance tickets
        and quay-to-temple transfers at Edfu and Kom Ombo are included.
25. At Edfu, what transport do you provide from the quay to the temple? We require
    minibuses, NOT horse-drawn carriages.
26. Is a Russian-speaking Egyptologist available through you, or must we bring our own?

Events on board
27. Can we arrange a gala dinner on board with decoration, special menu and live
    performers? What is charged extra, and where and when can performers board?
28. Crew gratuities: what do you recommend, and how is it normally handled?

Please send your written reply with a reference number for the enquiry. We intend to
decide by mid-October 2026.

Kind regards,
Ksenia
La Royal Event, Egypt
```
